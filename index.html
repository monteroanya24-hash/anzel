About

Customer-first strategy, grounded in real conversations.

I didn't start in marketing — I started on the phones, in the chats, and behind the desks where real customer problems live. That's my edge: I design campaigns that sound like humans, because I've spent thousands of hours listening to them.

My background crosses three worlds: customer operations, executive support, and digital marketing. Working as a technical support rep at VXI, a retail rep at Amazon, and a chat rep at Ibex taught me how customers actually think, complain, and decide — the kind of insight you can't learn from a dashboard.

As a personal assistant at GoGreen Manufacturer, I learned how real businesses operate end-to-end — how procurement, production, and communication have to align for anything to move. That operational literacy now shapes the way I think about funnels and go-to-market plans.

Today, at CodeWeb as an SEO specialist, I bring all of this together: I research markets, map customer intent, design content and funnel structures, and measure what actually moves the needle.
